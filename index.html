The distance has only made me more certain: you are my home. I want you to know that no matter what happens in our life, in any moment, no matter how many miles stand between us, I will love you forever. I will love you until the end, because nothing and no one can take you away from me. They say couples stay together "until death do them part", but I believe that nothing can break us — not time zones, not a single kilometer. I believe we are meant to be together forever, and I am and always will be 100% ready to fight for you, for us, for every virtual hug and every late night call. Still, I hope you understand how much I love you, how much I care and would sacrifice myself for your wellbeing, even from afar. I really want you to know you're the most special thing that has ever happened to me, you're the one who makes all of my hopes and dreams come true, and the one who makes my heart melt every time you simply smile on a video call. I love you, and no matter how many times I say this, it will never change. I love you today, yesterday and forever — across every distance. Thank you once again for loving me so much, for spending so much time with me even when we can't be in the same room, for caring and worrying about me from wherever you are. I love you truly, more than anything that exists, and I will always be with you — in every heartbeat, every text, every "goodnight." Thank you for being you, the perfect girl that you are, and for making long distance feel like the shortest path to forever. I love you.
